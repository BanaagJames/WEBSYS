B.S. Information Technology

ABOUT I.T.

Discover a future-focused program designed to equip you with real-world technical skills across networking, development, and intelligent automation.

What is BS Information Technology?

The Bachelor of Science in Information Technology is a four-year program that trains students to become highly skilled in the practical and technical aspects of computer systems, networking, troubleshooting, and hardware repair.

At STI College Calamba, the BSIT program is delivered through three specialized tracks — each designed to align with the demands of today's technology industry and equip graduates with career-ready skills from day one.

Specialization Tracks

Choose the path that matches your passion and career goals.
